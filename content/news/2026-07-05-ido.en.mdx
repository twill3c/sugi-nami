The well came into summer two degrees warmer than last year.

Mizu-yōkan is boiled and then chilled until it sets, and how fast it chills
changes how it feels in the mouth. Two degrees is enough to make it a different
sweet.

## Is there a reason not to use a refrigerator

Honestly, a refrigerator chills faster and more evenly. We use the well because
**the well is slower**. Setting slowly leaves a clearer cut face.

Chilled fast, the agar contracts and pushes water out, and a line of fine
bubbles appears along the cut. It eats the same. It does not look the same.

## What we are doing about it this year

We have cut each batch by a third. A smaller batch chills faster, which puts
some of the two degrees back. To make up for it we boil twice a day. It does
mean we are selling out in the afternoon more often. Our apologies.
