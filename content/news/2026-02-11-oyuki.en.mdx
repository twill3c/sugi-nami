We were closed for three days, the eighth to the tenth. We shut without being
able to tell anyone, and we are sorry for it.

Sixty centimetres fell overnight around Togakushi Chūsha. The prefectural road
was stopped on the climb and the morning bus did not run.

## Why snow days cannot be posted in advance

The opening-days calendar carries only the weekly pattern and the fixed annual
closures. Snow days cannot go on it.

The decision gets made the evening before, watching how it is falling and
whether the bus will make the climb in the morning. If it will not, it is not
only that customers cannot get here. I cannot get here either.

If you are coming a long way in winter, please
[look at the forecast the day before](/en/calendar) before you decide.

## Snow also puts a sweet on the list

We opened from midday on the eleventh. The hearth was lit, so the snow-viewing
dango was being grilled.

That sweet can only be served on a snowy day, because the hearth is only lit on
a snowy day. It is a poor thing to say after closing for three days — but it is
also a sweet we could only make because it snowed.
