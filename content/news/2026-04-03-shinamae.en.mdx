We are back. Open as usual from the fourth.

The shop was closed for four days from the end of March. That is the break we
take to bring the winter sweets down and put the spring ones up.

## Why it takes four days

Swapping the sweets over takes a day. What takes the rest is the testing
afterwards.

The same recipe is not the same sweet once the flour changes. Through winter we
were milling last autumn's grain; from April the same grain has been resting for
half a year. Rested grain takes up water differently, which means going back to
the beginning on how firmly the sobagaki is kneaded.

## What changed

- The zenzai has gone back to coarse bean paste, from the winter's smooth shiruko
- The snow-viewing dango is finished. The hearth gets its yearly clean in May
- The mugwort kusamochi has started. It runs only while the mugwort can be picked

Over the winter we closed for snow on seven days. Two more than last year.
