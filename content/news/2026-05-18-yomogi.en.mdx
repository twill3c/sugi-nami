The mugwort kusamochi comes off the list this Sunday.

Mugwort toughens as it grows. It can be picked for about four weeks, from the
first shoots until it stands twenty centimetres or so. After that the fibres set,
and no amount of steaming gets them out of your teeth.

## Eight days early this year

The second half of April was warm and it grew fast. Every time we go out to
pick, a slope that worked last week no longer does.

There is a little left on the north-facing slopes, but the scent there is
thinner. Rather than stretch the season by mixing in the thin stuff, we are
stopping while it is still strong.

## Until next year

The same sweet returns next April. Until then the
[cherry-leaf buckwheat dorayaki](/en/menu) stays on until the end of May.

We could freeze what we pick and serve it all year. We do not. Thawed mugwort
keeps its colour and loses its scent, which means the sweet would be telling a
lie about itself.
